The method, without the mystique

Informed by seeds.
Not a crystal ball.

Chalk takes the better seed, with a coin flip for equal seeds. Balanced uses a simple seed-based curve. Chaos makes every matchup a 50/50 draw. These are playful heuristics, not calibrated forecasts.

The same field, starting picks, personality, and randomness seed produce the same bracket. Your manual picks always take priority when filling the rest.

The starting field is an archived 2025 men’s tournament, after the First Four. It is not a live feed. Edit the field to use your own 64 teams. View the official historical bracket ↗
